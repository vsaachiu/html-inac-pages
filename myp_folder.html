What is Your ✶MYP Folder?

Your *MYP folder is your dedicated digital home across all of secondary school at VSA. Inside, you will find pre-created folders for each of your subjects—ready for your class notes, lab reports, design folios, and creative presentations.

Shared with Your Teachers for Real-Time Coaching

Your teachers have access to celebrate your progress and drop in timely feedback, encouragement, and guidance while you draft—without waiting for final submissions.

Visible for Your Family to Celebrate Your Growth

Your parents have read-only visibility into your active learning journey. Working transparently helps them support your goals, celebrate your inquiries, and partner in your growth.

Empowering Your ATL Self-Management: Embracing open, transparent work is the hallmark of a confident secondary learner. It builds accountability, pride in your process, and makes reflection second nature.
1 Find It

Locate Your Folder

Go to drive.google.com and search *MYP to find your folder named: *MYP [Your Name] - [ID]

2 Trackpad Tap

Add Shortcut

Two-finger tap on your MacBook trackpad (or right-click) on your folder and select: Organize → Add shortcut Or select your folder and press Shift+Z

3 Done

Select "My Drive"

Choose My Drive in the popup window and click Add. 🎉 Your *MYP folder will now sit right on your main Drive screen alongside your daily files!

4 Quick Tips for MYP Success

How to use your folders like an organized IB learner.

Bookmark it on Day 1

Open your folder once in Safari or Chrome and press Cmd+D on your MacBook to pin it to your favorites bar. Instant 1-click access all year!

Practice "Save-It-Right"

Starting a new essay, lab report, or slide deck? Open your specific subject folder (e.g. Sciences or Design) first, then click + New. No more loose files!

No "Share" Button Needed

Anything you create inside your subject folders is automatically visible to your subject teachers. You never have to copy/paste share links or answer "Request Access" emails.

One Home for All Your School Work

Use these folders for all your classes and keep everything organized by subject. When every draft, inquiry note, and project lives in its dedicated subject space, revising for tests and tracking assignments becomes effortless.
